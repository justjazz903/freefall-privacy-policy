# Privacy Policy for freefall

Last updated: September 21, 2026

freefall (referred to as "the App") is built and maintained as an offline freediving and apnea training application. This service is provided as-is.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
The App is a **purely offline utility application**. Because the App operates strictly on your local device without any external network dependency:
*   **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, locations, or usage statistics.
*   **Offline Operation:** The App runs entirely local to your device and does not require internet access or network permissions to function.

### 2. Device Permissions
The App does not require access to any sensitive permissions on your device (such as your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or photo library).

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, track-sharing providers, or advertising networks that collect, monitor, or track user information.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Since the App does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any modifications are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.
